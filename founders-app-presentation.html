Functions

What members can do

Six main things the app lets founders do.

✨

5 picks every day

Each morning the app suggests 5 founders worth meeting, based on what you need and what they offer.

🔎

Search founders

Filter by industry, city, company stage or what they're looking for.

🤝

Connect with a note

Send a personal request. A weekly limit keeps requests thoughtful.

💬

Private chat

Message your connections one-to-one, with notifications.

🙋

Ask the community

Post a need like "looking for a CTO" and get help from members.

🛡️

Safety controls

Report or block anyone. Delete your account and data any time.

Behind the scenes

What powers the app

Reliable, well-known services so we can build quickly and keep members safe.

📱

One app, two phones

Built once and works on both Android and iPhone.

☁️

Secure servers in India

Member data is stored safely within India.

💳

Trusted payments

Google Play, App Store and Razorpay (UPI & cards).

🏛️

Official records

Founder details are checked against government company data.

For the tech team: Flutter · Supabase (Mumbai) · RevenueCat · Razorpay · MCA verification API · Firebase notifications
